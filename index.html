Sequential 7-Agent Analysis Ingestion Pipeline

1

Agent 1: Collector (Dynamic Source Ingestion)

Gathers exactly 1,000 customer review records across 6 platform channels (Reddit, Play Store, App Store, YouTube, Twitter, Quora) enforcing target counts to ensure representative sample distribution.

2

Agent 2: Cleaner (Hinglish Translating & Deduplication)

Translates Indian Hinglish retail slang (e.g. mehanga, sasta, bekar), normalizes HTML/emojis, filters duplicate entries, and excludes commercial or promotional spam.

3

Agent 3: Analyzer (Behavioral Profiling)

Performs detailed natural language extraction on each individual text to discover buying triggers, shopping segment cohorts, barrier points, and categories mentioned.

4

Agent 4: Clusterer (Vector Space Clustering)

Applies TF-IDF vectorization and unsupervised K-Means clustering algorithm to group customer reviews into 4 semantic barrier themes.

5

Agent 5: Synthesizer (PM Synthesis)

Aggregates the quantitative breakdowns and semantic clusters to formulate synthesized answers to the 8 core PM discovery questions.

6

Agent 6: Validator (Dynamic Validation Engine)

Programmatically verifies every synthesized answer by performing keyword-based cohort count validation against database entries, calculating a quantitative validation confidence score, and extracting supporting and contradicting quotes.

7

Agent 7: PM Recommendation (ICE Prioritization)

Translates validated insights into prioritized feature opportunity briefs scored under the ICE model (Impact, Confidence, Ease).
